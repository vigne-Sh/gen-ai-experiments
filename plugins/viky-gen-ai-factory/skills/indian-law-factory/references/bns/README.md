# Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 — full text with notes

All **358 sections** of the BNS, one file per chapter, with this skill's notes under each section.
Use it to quote a section exactly, and to find its old IPC number. For the plain-language guide to
common offences, start with [`../bns-ipc-key-offences.md`](../bns-ipc-key-offences.md).

**How to look up a section:** search for `### BNS 303 ` (with the trailing space) across this folder,
or use the table below to open the right chapter file.

## Read this before relying on it

- **Text as enacted.** It is the Gazette text of 25 December 2023 (in force from 1 July 2024). Any
  later amendment is **not** shown — check [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) for the current text.
- **Machine-extracted.** The text was pulled from the Gazette PDF automatically. Paragraph breaks and
  the side-headings may be imperfect; the words themselves were not edited. For a court filing, use a
  certified or official copy.
- **The notes are not part of the Act.** They are this skill's own work: the old IPC equivalent and,
  for about 80 common offences, a plain-words summary from `../bns-ipc-key-offences.md`.
- **Old IPC numbers come from two places, always labelled:**
  - *Official chart* — the comparative chart in the Punjab Police Academy handbook published by BPR&D
    (100 rows, commonly used sections only).
  - *Wording match* — the IPC section whose words this BNS section carries over, found by comparing
    the two texts (share of the IPC section's four-word phrases found in the BNS section, minimum 50%;
    each IPC section is assigned to one BNS section only). Tested against the 100 chart rows, it found
    75 and gave **no wrong pairing**; it misses short punishment clauses and the sections added to the
    IPC in 2013, which the MHA IPC copy does not contain.
  - Where neither exists, the note says so — never guess an IPC number from it.
- **Cross-check of the skill's own table:** every IPC→BNS pair in `../bns-ipc-key-offences.md` that
  the chart or the wording match covers agrees with them (0 disagreements, checked 2026-10-13).

## Why publishing this text is allowed

Copyright Act 1957, s.52(1)(q)(ii) says reproducing an Act of a legislature is not infringement when it
is published **together with commentary or other original matter**. Every section here carries this
skill's notes (IPC equivalents, the method above, plain-words summaries). How much commentary is
enough has not been settled by a court, so if the Government asks for removal, remove the text and
keep the notes.
