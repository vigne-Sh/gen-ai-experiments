## Appeals and penalty (checked 2026-10-13)

| Provision | What it says | Checked against |
|---|---|---|
| s.19(1) | **First appeal** to an officer senior to the PIO, in the same public authority, within **30 days** of the reply (or of the date a reply was due) | [Goa SIC order](https://gsic.goa.gov.in/olddata/Appl7-2006-Sect.pdf), [TN SIC second-appeal guidelines](https://www.tnsic.gov.in/pdf/circulars/Second_appeal_guidelines.pdf) |
| s.19(3) | **Second appeal** to the Central or State Information Commission within **90 days** of the first-appeal decision; late appeals allowed for sufficient cause | Same, and [CIC office order](https://cic.gov.in/sites/default/files/Circulars%20%26Noification/Office%20Order%2014-06-2018_0.pdf) |
| s.20 | Penalty on a PIO who refuses or delays without reasonable cause: **₹250 per day, up to ₹25,000**. **Only the Information Commission** can impose it — not the first appellate authority | Commission orders above for who imposes; the ₹250/₹25,000 figures from a secondary source ([Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/current-affairs/rti-second-appeals-central-information-commission-appcoms/?pdf=1)) — confirm in the Act text |
