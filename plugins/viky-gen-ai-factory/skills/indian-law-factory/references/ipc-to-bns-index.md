# IPC → BNS index (every IPC section in the MHA copy)

For an offence committed **before 1 July 2024** the IPC still applies; for one on or after that date,
the BNS. This index gives the BNS home of each IPC section so both numbers can be quoted.

- **Headings** are from the Ministry of Home Affairs IPC copy (SHA-256 `f25d6154…`), which **predates
  the 2013 Criminal Law (Amendment) Act**: sections 326A, 326B, 354A–354D, 370A, 376AB, 376DA and 376DB
  are missing from it, and 370, 375, 376 and 376A–376E show their **pre-2013 headings**. Rows for the
  missing sections below come from the official chart only.
- **BNS column:** *(official chart)* = Punjab Police Academy handbook chart published by BPR&D;
  *(wording match)* = automated text comparison, see [`bns/README.md`](bns/README.md) for the method
  and its tested accuracy. **—** means neither source gives a mapping: look it up live, never guess.
- Full BNS text with notes: [`bns/`](bns/README.md).
