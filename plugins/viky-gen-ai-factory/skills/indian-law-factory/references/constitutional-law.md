# Constitutional law — references

**Last checked:** 2026-09-30

## Framework
Constitution of India, 1950. Fundamental rights in Part III. Writ
jurisdiction: Article 32 (Supreme Court), Article 226 (High Courts).

## Landmark cases (verified citations — case law is exactly where a
fabricated citation does the most damage, so these were checked live,
not recalled)

| Case | Citation | Court / date | Holding | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala | (1973) 4 SCC 225 (also AIR 1973 SC 1461) | Supreme Court, 13-judge bench, 24 April 1973, 7:6 majority | Established the **Basic Structure doctrine** — Parliament can amend any part of the Constitution under Art. 368, but cannot alter or destroy its "basic structure"/essential features | [iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/kbharatikerala/), [SCC Online blog](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2023/04/24/kesavananda-bharati-vs-state-of-kerala/) |
| Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India | AIR 1978 SC 597 | Supreme Court, 7-judge bench, 25 January 1978 | "Procedure" in Article 21 must be fair, just, and reasonable, not just any legislatively prescribed procedure; established the "Golden Triangle" reading of Articles 14, 19, and 21 together | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maneka_Gandhi_v._Union_of_India), [ipleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/maneka-gandhi-v-union-of-india/) |
| Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v.
