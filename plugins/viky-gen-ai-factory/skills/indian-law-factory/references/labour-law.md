This is the single highest-value entry in this whole reference
collection for catching a wrong answer: **check whether "today" (from
the user's perspective, not the model's training cutoff) is before or
after 21 November 2025** before saying which regime governs an
employment question. See `SKILL.md`'s live-research protocol section,
which was written specifically around this example.

## Unpaid wages under the Code on Wages, 2019 (checked 2026-10-13)

The main forum for a worker who has not been paid. From the Act text on a
reproduction site plus law-firm summaries, not the official Gazette copy
— confirm on India Code before relying on a number.

| Provision | What it says |
|---|---|
| s.17(2) | When a worker is removed, dismissed, retrenched or **resigns** (or the establishment closes), wages due must be paid within **two working days**. Covers all employees, whatever the salary. |
| s.45 | The government notifies an authority (not below a Gazetted Officer) to decide wage claims. The worker, a registered trade union, or the Inspector-cum-Facilitator can file. The authority can add compensation of up to **ten times** the amount claimed, and aims to decide within three months. |
| Time limit | A claim must be filed within **three years** of when it arose (summaries agree; section number not confirmed). |
| Inspector-cum-Facilitator | Replaces the old inspector; gives the employer a chance to comply before prosecution, and can file a claim for the worker. |

**What a worker will need to show:** that they worked there (appointment
letter, ID card, attendance, coworkers), the agreed wage (offer letter,
past salary slips or bank credits), and which months went unpaid. A
dismissal itself is challenged under the Industrial Relations Code,
2020 — its notice, retrenchment-compensation and dispute-filing rules
were **not** checked in this pass.

Checked against: [AdvocateKhoj s.17](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/codeonwages/17.php), [AdvocateKhoj s.45](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/codeonwages/45.php), [LiveLaw on two working days](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/two-working-days-code-wages-employee-final-settlement-552780), [AZB & Partners](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/the-code-on-wages-2019/), [Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/code-on-wages-2019-an-overview).
