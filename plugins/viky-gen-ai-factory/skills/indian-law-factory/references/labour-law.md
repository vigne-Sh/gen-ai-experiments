# Labour and employment law — references

**Last checked:** 2026-09-30

| Claim | Checked against |
|---|---|
| Four Labour Codes — Code on Wages (2019), Industrial Relations Code, Code on Social Security, Occupational Safety Health & Working Conditions (OSH) Code (2020) — consolidate roughly 29 prior central labour Acts (Factories Act 1948, Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Wages Act, etc.) | [PIB press release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192524&reg=3&lang=2), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/india-enforces-four-labour-codes-sweeps-away-29-outdated-laws-check-details-here) |
| The Codes were enacted/notified in 2019–2020 but **only actually came into force on 21 November 2025** — a multi-year gap between "enacted" and "in force" that a lot of pre-2025 commentary (and any model's stale training-data memory) will get wrong | Same sources |
| State and central rules under the Codes were still rolling out around the November 2025 commencement date | Same sources — treat any specific state's rule-notification status as needing a live check |

This is the single highest-value entry in this whole reference
collection for catching a wrong answer: **check whether "today" (from
the user's perspective, not the model's training cutoff) is before or
after 21 November 2025** before saying which regime governs an
employment question. See `SKILL.md`'s live-research protocol section,
which was written specifically around this example.
