# A to Z — key Indian laws an ordinary person should know exist

**Last checked:** 2026-10-05

**What this is:** a quick alphabetical map — "which law deals with what" — so a person who doesn't know where to start can find the right door. It is **not** every Indian law (there are hundreds of central Acts plus each state's own laws), and the one-line purposes are **general knowledge**, not freshly verified quotations. Laws get amended and replaced — always check the current text on [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) before relying on a detail. Entries already verified in this skill's other reference files say so and point to the file.

| Law | What it is for (plain words) |
|---|---|
| **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996** | Settling commercial disputes outside court through arbitration. Verified detail: `corporate-and-commercial-law.md` |
| **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023** | The criminal **procedure** code — FIR, arrest, bail, investigation, trial (replaced CrPC from 1 July 2024). `criminal-law.md`, `bns-ipc-key-offences.md`, `citizen-first-steps.md` |
| **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** | The main criminal **offences** code (358 sections; replaced the IPC, 511 sections, from 1 July 2024). `bns-ipc-key-offences.md` |
| **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** | The law of **evidence** — what can be proved in court and how (replaced the Evidence Act 1872). `criminal-law.md` |
| **Code of Civil Procedure, 1908** | How civil cases (money, property, contract disputes) are filed, tried and executed. |
| **Companies Act, 2013** | Forming and running companies; directors' duties. `corporate-and-commercial-law.md` |
| **Competition Act, 2002** | Stops cartels and abuse of market dominance. |
| **Constitution of India, 1950** | Fundamental rights (Part III), how government works; Articles 32 and 226 let you go straight to the Supreme Court/High Court for a rights violation. `constitutional-law.md` |
| **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** | Complaints about defective goods, poor service, unfair trade practices; District/State/National Commissions. `consumer-law.md` |
| **Copyright Act, 1957** | Protects books, music, films, software and similar creative work. `ip-law.md` |
| **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** | Rules for how organisations collect and use your personal data; consent, correction, erasure, a Data Protection Board. Parts of it are brought into force in stages — check which parts are in force today. |
| **Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961** | Makes giving, taking and demanding dowry an offence. |
| **Environment Protection Act, 1986** (and the Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981) | Pollution control and environmental protection. `environmental-law.md` |
| **Guardians and Wards Act, 1890** | Appointing a guardian for a minor's person or property; used in custody disputes. |
| **Hindu Marriage Act, 1955** | Marriage, divorce, judicial separation for Hindus (also Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs). |
| **Hindu Succession Act, 1956** (amended 2005) | Inheritance among Hindus; daughters are coparceners by birth since 2005. `family-and-succession-law.md` |
| **Income Tax Act, 1961** | Tax on income; rates change every year with the Finance Act. `tax-law.md` |
| **Indian Contract Act, 1872** | What makes an agreement a legally binding contract and what happens when it is broken. |
| **Indian Succession Act, 1925** | Wills and inheritance, mainly for non-Hindus and for wills generally. |
| **Information Technology Act, 2000** | Cyber offences (hacking, identity theft, obscene material), electronic records. `cyber-law.md` |
| **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016** | Time-bound resolution when a company or person cannot pay debts. `corporate-and-commercial-law.md` |
| **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015** | Children in conflict with law and children needing care and protection. |
| **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987** | Creates NALSA/State/District legal services authorities; **free legal aid**. `citizen-first-steps.md` |
| **Limitation Act, 1963** | Time limits for filing civil claims — wait too long and the claim can be barred. |
| **Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007** | Children/relatives' duty to maintain parents and senior citizens; tribunal route. |
| **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988** | Driving licences, vehicle rules, compulsory third-party insurance, accident compensation (MACT). `motor-vehicles-law.md` |
| **Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985** | Offences relating to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. |
| **Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881** | Cheques and promissory notes; s.138 bounced-cheque offence. `corporate-and-commercial-law.md` |
| **Patents Act, 1970** | Protects inventions — 20 years from filing. `ip-law.md` |
| **Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988** | Bribery and corruption by public servants. |
| **Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002** | Offence of laundering the proceeds of crime; enforced by the Enforcement Directorate. |
| **Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006** | Makes child marriage voidable and punishable. |
| **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012** | Sexual offences against anyone under 18; child-friendly procedure. |
| **Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005** | Civil protection for women facing domestic violence: protection orders, right to reside in the shared household, monetary relief. `family-and-succession-law.md` |
| **Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA)** | Regulates builders and promoters; protects home buyers. Not yet researched in `property-law.md`. |
| **Registration Act, 1908** | Which property documents must be registered. `property-law.md` |
| **Right to Information Act, 2005** | Ask any public authority for information; 30-day reply rule. `rti.md` |
| **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** | Rights, reservations and accessibility for persons with disabilities. |
| **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989** | Special offences and protections against atrocities on SC/ST members. |
| **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH)** | Internal Committees at workplaces; follows the *Vishaka* guidelines. `constitutional-law.md` |
| **Special Marriage Act, 1954** | Civil marriage between any two persons regardless of religion. |
| **Specific Relief Act, 1963** | Court orders to actually perform a contract, or injunctions, instead of just money. |
| **Trade Marks Act, 1999** | Protects brand names and logos — 10 years, renewable. `ip-law.md` |
| **Transfer of Property Act, 1882** | Rules for selling, mortgaging, leasing and gifting property. `property-law.md` |
| **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019** | Recognition and anti-discrimination rights for transgender persons. |

## Labour and tax in one line each
- **The four Labour Codes** (Wages 2019; Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety/Health/Working Conditions 2020) — replaced about 29 older Acts and came into force **21 November 2025**. `labour-law.md`
- **CGST Act, 2017 and state GST Acts** — GST, in force from 1 July 2017. `tax-law.md`

## Where to look for a law not on this list
Use India Code's search by Act name or keyword, the **e-Gazette** for notifications and commencement dates, and the **Legislative Department** site for the latest amendments. For state laws (land, rent control, police acts, local bodies), look at the **state government's** website — each state has its own.
