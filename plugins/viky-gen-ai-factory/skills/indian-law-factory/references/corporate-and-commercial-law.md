## Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016

| Claim | Checked against |
|---|---|
| The IBC created a unified Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), replacing prior fragmented debt-recovery laws, run by a resolution professional under the Adjudicating Authority (NCLT) with a committee of creditors | [TaxGuru IBC timelines](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/timelines-corporate-insolvency-resolution-process-insolvency-bankruptcy-code-2016.html) |
| CIRP must ordinarily complete within 180 days, extendable by 90 days with creditor majority approval, and must mandatorily conclude within 330 days including any extension/litigation period | Same source |

## Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (as amended 2015, 2019)

| Claim | Checked against |
|---|---|
| The 2015 amendment made a court-arbitral-award stay non-automatic on merely filing a s.34 challenge (ss.36, 87) | [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=8a8d0470-8b42-4778-b7e6-632c10f647c8), [VIA Mediation Centre](https://viamediationcentre.org/readnews/ODY=/The-Arbitration-and-Conciliation-Act-1996-The-2015-and-2019-Amendments) |
| The 2015 amendment gave the arbitral tribunal powers under s.17 equivalent to a court's s.9 interim-measure powers, enforceable as a court order | Same sources |
| The 2019 amendment empowers the Supreme Court (international commercial arbitration) and High Courts (domestic) to designate arbitral institutions for appointing arbitrators | Same sources |
