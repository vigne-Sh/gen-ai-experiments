# Property law — references

**Last checked:** 2026-09-30

| Claim | Checked against |
|---|---|
| Sale deeds of immovable property require compulsory registration under s.54 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 | [Legal Service India](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/registrable-documents-the-registration-act-1908/) |
| Mortgage instruments (s.59 TPA) and leases from year to year, or for a term exceeding one year, or reserving yearly rent, are also compulsorily registrable | Same source |
| Contracts for transfer of immovable property for consideration (s.53A TPA), executed on/after the Registration and Other Related Laws (Amendment) Act 2001, are compulsorily registrable | Same source |
| Under s.49 of the Registration Act 1908, a document required to be registered that isn't registered does not affect the immovable property it covers, and generally can't be used as evidence of the transaction | Same source |

Not independently verified in this pass: state-specific stamp duty
rates (these vary by state and change often — always check the current
state stamp Act/notification), gift-deed-specific registration
requirements beyond the general rule above, and each state's own RERA
rules. RERA itself is covered below.

## Landmark: a sale agreement, GPA or will is not a sale (verified 2026-10-05)

| Case | Citation | Court / date | Holding | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Haryana | (2012) 1 SCC 656 | Supreme Court, decided 11 October 2011 | Immovable property worth more than ₹100 can be sold only by a **registered deed of conveyance** (TPA s.54, Registration Act s.17); a sale agreement, general power of attorney or will, alone or together, does not transfer ownership | [LIJDLR](https://lijdlr.com/2026/04/29/case-law-analysis-suraj-lamp-industries-pvt-ltd-v-state-of-haryana-2012-1-scc-656/), [Delhi Law Academy](https://www.delhilawacademy.com/consti-suraj-lamp-case/) |

## RERA — Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (checked 2026-10-13)

For home buyers whose builder is late or breaks promises.

| Provision | What it says | Checked against |
|---|---|---|
| s.3 | A project must be registered with the state RERA if the land is **over 500 sq m or** it has **more than 8 apartments**. Regulators read "or" as either test being enough; the point has been argued in the Madras High Court, so check for your project | [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/rera-registration-mandatory-areas-more-than-500-sq-m/), [Goa RERA FAQ](https://rera.goa.gov.in/reraApp/resources/staticpage/FAQ%202%20(18.01.2023).pdf) |
| s.4(2)(l)(D) | **70%** of money collected from buyers goes into a separate bank account, used only for that project's land and construction | secondary summary ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/rera-registration.html)) — confirm in the Act |
| s.18 | If possession is late, the buyer may **withdraw and get the money back with interest plus compensation**, or **stay and get interest for every month of delay** until possession. Rate set by state rules (often SBI's highest MCLR + 2%) | [Haryana RERA order](https://haryanarera.gov.in/assistancecontrol/viewOrderPdf/OTA4MDg=), [Mohali Aerotropolis analysis](https://www.mohaliaerotropolis.com/article/298-builder-delayed-possession-in-mohali-what-rera-punjab-actually-awards-and-how-long-it-really-takes) |
| s.31 | Any aggrieved person can file a complaint with the state RERA Authority. After the Supreme Court's *Newtech Promoters* ruling, **refund and interest** are decided by the Authority, **compensation** by the Adjudicating Officer (s.71) | Same |

**What a buyer will need to show:** the registered agreement for sale
(with the promised possession date), payment receipts and bank
statements, the builder's letters, and the project's RERA registration
number (look it up on the state RERA website).
