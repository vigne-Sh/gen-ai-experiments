# Cyber / IT law — references

**Last checked:** 2026-09-30

## Information Technology Act, 2000 (as amended)

| Section | Covers | Punishment (as found — verify current figure live, amendments happen) | Checked against |
|---|---|---|---|
| s.43 | Unauthorized access to / damage of a computer resource (civil compensation provision) | Compensation, not imprisonment, under this section | [ApniLaw](https://www.apnilaw.com/legal-articles/acts/section-66c-of-the-it-act-identity-theft-in-the-digital-age/) and surrounding commentary |
| s.66 | Hacking (dishonest/fraudulent unauthorized access) | Up to 3 years imprisonment and/or fine up to ₹5 lakh | Same |
| s.66C | Identity theft (fraudulent use of another person's password/electronic signature/unique identifying feature) | Up to 3 years imprisonment and/or fine up to ₹1 lakh | Same |
| s.67 | Publishing/transmitting obscene material in electronic form | Up to 5 years imprisonment and/or fine up to ₹10 lakh (first conviction figures) | Same |

**Not independently verified in this pass:** s.66D (cheating by
