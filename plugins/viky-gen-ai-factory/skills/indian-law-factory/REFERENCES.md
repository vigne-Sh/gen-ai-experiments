## Verified facts for the newly added domains (2026-09-30 pass)

| Domain | Claim | Checked against |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer protection | Consumer Protection Act 2019 replaced the 1986 Act, in force July 2020; three-tier Commissions — District (claims up to ₹1 crore), State (₹1 crore-₹10 crore), National (above ₹10 crore) | [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/876600/the-consumer-protection-act-2019-an-overview), [LegalOnus](https://legalonus.com/consumer-protection-act-2019-vs-1986-what-has-changed/) — pecuniary thresholds can be revised by notification, re-check before citing a figure |
| Cyber / IT law | IT Act 2000: s.43 unauthorized access, s.66 hacking (up to 3 yrs and/or ₹5 lakh fine), s.66C identity theft (up to 3 yrs and/or ₹1 lakh fine), s.67 publishing obscene material electronically (up to 5 yrs and/or ₹10 lakh fine, first offence) | [ApniLaw](https://www.apnilaw.com/legal-articles/acts/section-66c-of-the-it-act-identity-theft-in-the-digital-age/), search-aggregated summary citing IT Act text — punishment figures can change on amendment, re-verify before citing |
| RTI | RTI Act 2005 s.7 timelines: 30 days standard, 48 hours where life or liberty is concerned, 40 days if third-party consultation is needed; s.8(1) has 10 exemption grounds | [Section 7 - righttoinformation.wiki](https://righttoinformation.wiki/act/section-7), [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/rti-judgement-series-you-can-get-answers-within-48-hours-if-it-is-a-question-of-life-or-liberty/29811.html) |
| Corporate / company law | Companies Act 2013 commenced in phases (98 sections from 12 Sept 2013, remainder from 1 Apr 2014); the 1956 Act was formally repealed 30 Jan 2019 | [TaxGuru comparative analysis](https://taxguru.in/company-law/comparative-analysis-companies-act-2013-companies-act-1956.html) |
| Tax — GST | CGST Act 2017 received assent 12 April 2017, GST went live nationwide 1 July 2017 | [CBIC](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/documents/Central_Goods_and_Services_Tax_Act__2017_28-September-2022.html), [PRS Legislative Research](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-central-goods-and-services-tax-bill-2017) |
| Labour / employment | The 4 Labour Codes (Wages 2019; Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety/Health/Working Conditions 2020) consolidate ~29 prior Acts, but only actually **came into force 21 November 2025** despite being notified/enacted years earlier — a real trap for anyone (human or model) answering from an older knowledge snapshot | [PIB press release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192524&reg=3&lang=2), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/india-enforces-four-labour-codes-sweeps-away-29-outdated-laws-check-details-here) |
