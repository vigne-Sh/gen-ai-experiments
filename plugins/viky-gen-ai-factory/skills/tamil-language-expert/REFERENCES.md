## Not independently verified — carried over from the original author

The plugin's own original README said this plainly, and it stays true
after folding the skill into this marketplace: **the joining-consonant
section (வலி மிகுதல், section 5) and the legal-vocabulary list (section
8) were written from general knowledge and have not been checked against
an authoritative Tamil grammar reference or by a Tamil linguist.**

That covers most of sections 4-8: the specific consonant-doubling
triggers/exceptions, the formal-word spelling list, agreement examples,
and the petitioner/respondent/advocate-style legal vocabulary. `SKILL.md`
itself already builds in the right response to this — never claim
certainty on these, list them as items for a fluent human reader to
check, and point to Tamil Virtual Academy / Tamil Nadu government
glossaries / a human reader (section 10) rather than asserting them as
settled. If you know Tamil well and can confirm or correct any of this,
that's exactly the kind of correction worth making directly.
