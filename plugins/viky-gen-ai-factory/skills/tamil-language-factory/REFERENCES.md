## Tamil script: 247 letters

12 vowels (உயிரெழுத்து), 18 consonants (மெய்யெழுத்து), 216 vowel+consonant
combinations (உயிர்மெய்யெழுத்து), and 1 additional character ஃ (ஆய்த
எழுத்து, āyta eḻuttu) = 247 total.

Checked against: [Wikipedia - Tamil script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_script), [easytamiltyping.com](https://www.easytamiltyping.com/tamil/letters), [Discover Tamil - alphabet chart](https://discovertamil.com/resources/tamil-alphabet)

## Tolkāppiyam — oldest extant Tamil grammar

Author traditionally given as Tolkāppiyar. Its date is a genuine,
unresolved scholarly dispute — proposals in the sources checked range
from c. 300 BCE up to the 3rd century CE (Encyclopaedia Britannica dates
it to the 1st–4th century CE). Don't state a single date as settled fact;
say the dating is disputed and give the range.

Checked against: [Wikipedia - Tolkāppiyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolk%C4%81ppiyam), [Britannica - Tolkappiyam](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Tolkappiyam)

## Tamil declared a Classical Language of India (2004)

Tamil was the first language declared a "Classical Language" by the
Government of India, via a notification dated **12 October 2004**. Which
ministry issued it is reported inconsistently across the sources
checked (some say Ministry of Home Affairs, others Ministry of Human
Resource Development) — state the date and "Government of India"
confidently; if the specific ministry matters, verify it live rather
than repeating either claim as settled.

Checked against: [PIB press release (2004 classical-language criteria context)](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2061660), [Central Institute of Classical Tamil — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Institute_of_Classical_Tamil)
