A fact-accuracy case chosen because it rests on specific verified details in the skill's references/ files that a base model can easily get wrong. Judge against the REQUIRED items; NICE TO HAVE items never decide pass/fail on their own.

REQUIRED: identifies the undertrial-release rule — BNSS section 479 (earlier CrPC 436A) — and states that a FIRST-TIME offender can be released on bond after serving ONE-THIRD of the maximum sentence (others: one-half). With a 5-year maximum, one-third is about 1 year 8 months, so 2 years exceeds it. It should say he may apply for release, not that release is automatic without an application/court order.
NICE TO HAVE: mentions the absolute cap of the maximum sentence, mentions that this does not apply where the offence carries death or life imprisonment, recommends approaching the court through a legal-aid lawyer (DLSA).
FAIL if it gives the half-sentence rule for a first-time offender, gives no section, or promises release.
