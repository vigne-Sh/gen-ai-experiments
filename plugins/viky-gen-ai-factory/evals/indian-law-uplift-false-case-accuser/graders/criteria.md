A fact-accuracy case chosen because it rests on specific verified details in the skill's references/ files that a base model can easily get wrong. Judge against the REQUIRED items; NICE TO HAVE items never decide pass/fail on their own.

REQUIRED: identifies false charge made with intent to injure — IPC 211 / BNS 248 — as the main section, and may also mention giving false information to a public servant (IPC 182 / BNS 217) or false evidence. Both the old and new numbers should be given for whichever it names.
NICE TO HAVE: explains that proceedings against the accuser need the facts to show they KNEW the charge was false and had intent to injure, that this is normally begun through the court/proper procedure rather than the police alone, and recommends legal aid or a lawyer.
FAIL if it names clearly wrong sections (for example defamation only, with no mention of a false-charge or false-information offence) or states the accuser will definitely be punished.
