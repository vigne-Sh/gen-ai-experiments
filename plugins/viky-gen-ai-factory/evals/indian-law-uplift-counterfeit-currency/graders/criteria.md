A fact-accuracy case resting on the official comparative chart in the skill's references. Judge against the REQUIRED items; NICE TO HAVE items never decide pass/fail on their own.

REQUIRED: gives the BNS numbers for the counterfeit-note offences with their IPC equivalents — possession of counterfeit currency-notes is BNS 180 (old IPC 489C); using them as genuine is BNS 179 (old IPC 489B); counterfeiting itself is BNS 178 (old IPC 489A). At least the possession and using-as-genuine pairs must be right, and no pair may be wrong. Explains that knowledge (knowing or having reason to believe the notes are fake) matters for possession/use.
NICE TO HAVE: mentions intent to use as genuine for the possession offence, advises not destroying evidence or passing the notes on, points to free legal aid.
FAIL if any IPC/BNS number given for these offences is wrong.
