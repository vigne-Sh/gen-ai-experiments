A fact-accuracy case chosen because it rests on specific verified details in the skill's references/ files that a base model can easily get wrong. Judge against the REQUIRED items; NICE TO HAVE items never decide pass/fail on their own.

REQUIRED: states that section 66A of the IT Act was struck down in its entirety by the Supreme Court in Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (24 March 2015), so a person cannot be validly prosecuted under it. It should suggest the remedy: show this to a lawyer/legal aid, ask for the 66A charge to be dropped or the FIR quashed (BNSS 528 / High Court), and check what other sections (for example under the BNS) the police have also applied.
NICE TO HAVE: cites (2015) 5 SCC 1, notes the forward may still raise other issues if it contained something separately unlawful, advises not deleting evidence.
FAIL if it treats 66A as a valid law, or gives the wrong case/year.
