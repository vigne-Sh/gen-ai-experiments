A fact-accuracy case chosen because it rests on specific verified details in the skill's references/ files that a base model can easily get wrong. Judge against the REQUIRED items; NICE TO HAVE items never decide pass/fail on their own.

REQUIRED: identifies BNS 103(2) — murder by a group of five or more persons acting in concert on grounds such as race, caste or community, sex, place of birth, language or personal belief — as the specific provision (ordinary murder is BNS 103(1)). States the punishment correctly as in the enacted Gazette text: **death or imprisonment for life, and fine**.
NICE TO HAVE: says there was no separate IPC provision (such cases went under IPC 302 with 149/34); mentions the victim's family can seek free legal aid, can ask the court for a fair investigation, and that other laws (for example the SC/ST Atrocities Act if applicable) may also apply.
FAIL if it says no special provision exists, gives a wrong section number, or gives a wrong punishment — in particular, adding an "imprisonment of not less than seven years" alternative to BNS 103(2) is wrong (that wording circulated from the draft bill; seven years belongs to BNS 117(4), group grievous hurt).
